# CORE 01 — Parents and fusion

## Parents

| Parent | Irreducible contribution to CEK |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| **ux-channel** | Intent under Cap; Result of Ops; Host/Peer; Baseline; correlation ≠ authority; fail closed |
| **Cordis** | Bounded composition with lifetime; Context mediation; inject; structural limit/isolate; reversible caused change |

## Fusion principle

One law, not two stacked frameworks.  
Parent-local APIs and brand words are discarded; intentions are kept.

## Concept lineage (informative)

| CEK concept | Primary ancestry |
|-------------|------------------|
| Intent, Cap, Ops, Result, Host, Peer, Baseline, profile, trace | ux-channel line |
| Activity, Context, inject, limit, isolate, part, lineage, reverse | Cordis line (renamed/clarified) |

**Activity** replaces unclear “Fiber”.  
**Baseline** replaces unclear “Floor”.  
**trace** replaces correlation formerly discussed as flow (explicitly non-authority).  
**limit** is the public restriction verb; **isolate** remains the structural Context-slice operation.
